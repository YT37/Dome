How to read Dome's results

Dome turns a set of already-flagged social accounts into a short, explained list of buildings where a group's photos were most likely taken. Every result is a lead for a human to verify, never a finding.

1The six agents

A supervisor runs six agents in order (Spotter, Linker with money, Zoner, Lister, Locator, Briefer). Each writes one JSON file into the case folder and a line into the log. The supervisor decides the next step from the data, and every number comes from deterministic code.

LinkerWhich accounts are one ring? → rings.json
ZonerWhere, as an area? → zones.json
ListerWhich buildings fit? → candidates.json
LocatorWhich room and window view match? → matches.json
BrieferHow strong, and why? → brief.json

2Reading the graph

Each dot is a flagged account. Grey lines are reposts, replies and follows. When the Linker finishes, the ring lights up.

  • Ring members are linked by at least two different kinds of public key, for example the same phone in ads and the same reworded ad script.
  • Recruiters have agency or job-offer bios and send most of their reposts into the ring.
  • Hard negatives stay grey. Rental hosts reuse listing photos, a spa chain shares one script, friends repost each other. Each shares only one kind of key, so Dome never links them. That restraint is the point.
  • Dashed lines are weak hints: one kind of key only, kept for the analyst and never counted as a link.

3Topology view

A normal graph draws lines between two accounts. Coordination happens in groups, so the Linker's evidence is also built as a shape: accounts are rank 0, and every key shared by two or more accounts is a rank 1 cell that holds all of them at once (one phone, one script, one reposted photo).

A topological neural network (UniGCN) reads that shape and gives each account a probability of being in a ring. Dome reports its average over the ring as the topological score, next to the rule-based confidence R. It is a second opinion: the rules make every link and write every reason, and the model never changes a tier.

In the heatmap, the ring is a block of dense rows touching several kinds of cell. A hard negative's row lights up in one column only.

4The dome

The Zoner turns the place clues in the ring's ads (stated areas, landmarks, area codes) into an area. Each clue spreads its weight over a 1 km grid; the smallest set of cells holding 80% of the weight is the dome.

ShownMeaning
Area (km²)How large the search area is. Smaller is better.
Weight shareShare of all place-clue weight inside this zone.
Confidence Zweight share ÷ (1 + area ÷ 10). Large, vague areas score low.
WideOver 25 km², or mostly city-wide clues. The Lister then keeps only buildings with a 3D scan.

Place clues alone never make a lead. Street labels are hidden on the map, and no address is ever shown.

5The five factors

FactorQuestionFrom
R ringHow sure is it that these accounts act together?Linker
Z zoneHow tight is the area the building sits in?Zoner
F fitDoes the building's type and height fit the photos?Lister
P photoDo the ad photos match rooms in this building's 3D scan?Locator
W windowDoes the view from the window match what is outside the building? "Skipped" when no ad shows a window.Locator

Score = 100 × R × (0.15 Z + 0.10 F + 0.45 P + 0.30 W). Without W, the other weights are rescaled. The score only orders leads within a tier.

6Tiers and caps

TierRule
HighP ≥ 0.6, W ≥ 0.6 and R ≥ 0.6: the room and the window view both match a strong ring.
MediumA strong room match (P ≥ 0.6) where something else falls short, or a middling match (0.4 ≤ P < 0.6) with a strong window view.
LowEverything else.

A cap line says exactly why a lead is not higher, for example "no window photo, window check skipped". If any pipeline step fails, every lead is capped at Low and the failed step is named.

7The decision log

Every tool call and result is a line in log.jsonl. Highlighted rows are supervisor decisions that depend on the data. Click one to see the evidence that triggered it. Examples:

  • scanned_buildings_only_wide_zone: the zone is wide, so only buildings with a 3D scan are kept.
  • skip_window_check_cap_medium: no ad photo shows a window, so W is skipped and leads cap at Medium.

Red rows are failed or refused steps. Each step retries once before it is marked failed.

8Verify next

Each lead lists what a human should check next: which records to request through legal process, and what would confirm or rule the lead out. Dome stops at the brief.

9What Dome is not

  • Not evidence of anyone's presence. A lead is a place to check, nothing more.
  • Not an address finder. Areas, not addresses; buildings appear by display name only.
  • Not a contact tool. Nothing here messages, dispatches or publishes anything, and there is no chat.
